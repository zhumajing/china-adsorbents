Typical Applications

  • Removal of impurities such as H₂O, CO₂ and acetylene in the raw material gas of air purification system and separation of normal isomerization alkanes.
  • Separation of normal and isomeric alkanes (C4-C6 fractions) in 2-paraffin industry.
  • Deep drying and purification of air, O₂, N₂, H₂ and mixed gases.
  • Purification and drying of petroleum and natural gas, ammonia decomposition gas and other industrial gases and liquids.
  • Purification and separation of inert gases.
  • PSA for hydrogen production.
