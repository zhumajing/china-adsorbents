13X Molecular Sieve JZ‑ZMS9 — Technical Data Sheet

Sodium aluminosilicate; pore opening ≤ 9 Å.
Description: JZ-ZMS9 is a high-purity 13X molecular sieve with a large pore size (9Å) and high surface area, designed for the efficient removal of CO₂, H₂O, H₂S, and mercaptans. It is widely used in air separation pre-purification, natural gas sweetening, and deep drying of industrial gases.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Large pore size (9Å) enables adsorption of larger molecules including CO₂, H₂S, mercaptans, and heavier hydrocarbons.
  • Very high static water adsorption capacity (≥26.5%) and CO₂ adsorption (≥17.5%) for superior purification.
  • Excellent crushing strength reduces dust and extends service life in PSA, TSA, and fixed-bed systems.
  • Low attrition rate (≤0.2%) minimizes pressure drop and maintenance costs.
  • Widely used in air separation units (ASU) for removing CO₂ and moisture before cryogenic distillation.

Typical Applications

  • Purification of gas in air separation plants, removal of H₂O, CO₂ and hydrocarbons.
  • Dehydration and desulfurization (removal of H₂S and mercaptans) of natural gas, LNG, and liquid alkanes (propane, butane, etc.).
  • Deep drying of general gases (e.g. compressed air, permanent gas).
  • Drying and purification of synthetic ammonia.
  • Desulfurization and deodorization of aerosol.
  • CO₂ removal from pyrolysis gas and flue gas streams.

Typical Technical Specifications Summary

The JZ-ZMS9 13X molecular sieve has a nominal pore diameter of 9 angstroms (0.9 nm). Its equilibrium water adsorption capacity at 25°C and 60% RH is ≥26.5% by weight, and CO₂ adsorption at 25°C and 0.1 bar is ≥17.5%. Bulk density ranges from 0.62 to 0.64 g/ml depending on pellet shape. Crushing strength varies between 25 N/pellet (spherical 1.6–2.5 mm) and 80 N/pellet (spherical 3–5 mm). Attrition loss is ≤0.2%, and residual package moisture ≤1.5%. These properties meet international standards for 13X zeolite adsorbents used in air separation pre-purification, natural gas sweetening, and CO₂ removal.
