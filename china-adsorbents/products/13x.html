Typical Applications

  • Purification of gas in air separation plant, removal of H₂O, CO₂ and hydrocarbons.
  • Dehydration and desulfurization (removal of H₂S and mercaptan, etc.) of natural gas, LNG, and liquid alkanes (propane, butane, etc.).
  • Deep drying of general gases (e.g. compressed air, permanent gas).
  • Drying and purification of synthetic ammonia.
  • Desulfurization and deodorization of Aerosol.
  • CO₂ removal from pyrolysis gas.
