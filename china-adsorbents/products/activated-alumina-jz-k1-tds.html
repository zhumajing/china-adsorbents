Activated Alumina JZ‑K1 — Technical Data Sheet

Aluminium oxide (Al₂O₃). Description: JZ-K1 is a high-performance activated alumina desiccant with high adsorption capacity, stable surface structure, and outstanding mechanical strength. It is widely used in compressed air drying, PSA/VPSA systems, instrument air dryers, and as a catalyst carrier.

Key Features & Benefits

  • High specific surface area (280-300 m²/g) ensures excellent water adsorption capacity and fast kinetics.
  • High crush strength (up to 180 N/pc) reduces dusting and extends service life in thermal swing and pressure swing dryers.
  • Low attrition rate (≤0.3%) minimizes pressure drop and maintenance costs.
  • Uniform pore structure and high pore volume (≥0.4 ml/g) for efficient moisture removal down to -60°C dew point.
  • Chemically inert and resistant to thermal shocks, suitable for heatless and heated regeneration dryers.

Typical Applications

  • Desiccant and Dehydration: High-performance air drying, instrument air drying systems, and electronic components moisture control.
  • Industrial Equipment Compatibility: Ideal desiccant medium for heatless/heated compressed air dryers, air separation purifiers, PSA/VPSA systems, and high-purity nitrogen generators.
  • Chemical Carrier: Excellent choice as a robust catalyst carrier in various petrochemical processes, such as Claus sulfur recovery and fluoride removal.

Typical Technical Specifications Summary

The JZ-K1 activated alumina is a high-purity aluminum oxide (Al₂O₃) desiccant with a specific surface area ranging from 280 to 300 m²/g (BET method). Bulk density varies from 0.66 to 0.75 g/ml depending on particle size, with larger beads having lower density. Crush strength increases with particle size, ranging from 25 N/pellet (1.0-1.6 mm) up to 180 N/pellet (6-8 mm). Attrition loss is ≤0.3%, and loss on ignition (LOI) is ≤8%. These characteristics meet international standards for activated alumina used in compressed air dryers, PSA oxygen/nitrogen generators, and catalyst carrier applications.
