3A Molecular Sieve JZ‑ZMS3 — Technical Data Sheet

Potassium sodium aluminosilicate; pore opening ≤ 3 Å.
Description: JZ-ZMS3 is a high-purity 3A molecular sieve designed for selective adsorption of molecules with kinetic diameter ≤3Å. It offers exceptional water adsorption capacity and mechanical strength, making it the preferred desiccant for critical dehydration processes.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Uniform pore size (3Å) ensures precise molecular sieving, blocking molecules larger than 3 angstroms.
  • High static water adsorption capacity (≥21%) for deep drying down to -70°C dew point.
  • Excellent crushing strength reduces dust generation and extends service life in PSA/VSA units.
  • Low attrition rate (≤0.2%) minimizes bed pressure drop and maintenance costs.
  • Stable performance under harsh chemical environments and elevated temperatures.

Typical Applications

  • Used for drying unsaturated hydrocarbon gases such as ethylene, propylene, butadiene, etc.
  • Drying of polar liquids such as ethanol (anhydrous ethanol production).
  • Desiccant for deep drying, refining and polymerization of gas and liquid phase in petroleum and chemical industry.
  • Protection of olefin polymerization catalysts from moisture poisoning.

Typical Technical Specifications Summary

The JZ-ZMS3 3A molecular sieve exhibits a nominal pore diameter of 3 angstroms (0.3 nm). Its equilibrium water adsorption capacity at 25°C and 60% RH is ≥21% by weight. The bulk density ranges from 0.66 to 0.70 g/ml depending on pellet shape and size. Crushing strength varies between 25 N per pellet (spherical 1.6–2.5 mm) and 80 N per pellet (spherical 3–5 mm). Attrition loss is kept below 0.2%, and residual package moisture is ≤1.5%. These properties comply with industry standards for high-performance 3A zeolite adsorbents.
