Typical Applications

  • Catalyst Support & Bed Covering Layer: Widely used as the underlying support media and covering layer for catalysts in hydrocracking, ammonia synthesis, and reforming reactors.
  • Tower Packing & Mass Transfer: Acts as premium column packing and gas/liquid distribution media in refineries, petrochemical plants, and chemical processing facilities.
  • Thermal and Chemical Stability: Engineered to withstand extreme chemical corrosion, high temperatures, and severe mechanical stress under high-pressure environments.

Packaging Options

  • 25 KG Composite Paper Bags: Standard option for general industrial orders, easy to handle, store, and stack in warehouses.
  • 150 KG Galvanized Iron Drums: Highly recommended for long-term storage or transoceanic export shipments to provide ultimate protection against moisture, air ingress, and physical damage during transit.
