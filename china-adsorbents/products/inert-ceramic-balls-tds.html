Key Features & Benefits

  • High chemical purity and resistance to acids, alkalis, and organic solvents.
  • Excellent mechanical strength ensures minimal breakage and dust generation during transport and operation.
  • Low water absorption (≤5-8%) prevents moisture retention and catalyst poisoning.
  • Wide range of alumina content (20% to >90%) to suit different process temperatures and corrosive environments.
  • Smooth spherical shape provides uniform gas/liquid distribution and low pressure drop in packed beds.

Typical Applications

  • Catalyst Support & Bed Covering Layer: Widely used as the underlying support media and covering layer for catalysts in hydrocracking, ammonia synthesis, and reforming reactors.
  • Tower Packing & Mass Transfer: Acts as premium column packing and gas/liquid distribution media in refineries, petrochemical plants, and chemical processing facilities.
  • Thermal and Chemical Stability: Engineered to withstand extreme chemical corrosion, high temperatures (up to 1500°C for corundum grade), and severe mechanical stress under high-pressure environments.

Typical Technical Specifications Summary

The JZ-ICB inert ceramic balls are manufactured from high-purity alumina and silica. Five grades are available: Common Porcelain (Al₂O₃ 20-30%), Low Alumina (31-45%), Medium Alumina (46-70%), High Alumina (71-90%), and Corundum (>90%). Crushing strength varies by size and grade, ranging from 0.20 kN/pc (3mm common porcelain) to 25.0 kN/pc (76mm corundum). Water absorption is ≤5% for common and low alumina grades, and ≤8% for medium/high alumina and corundum. Acid and alkali resistance are ≥90% and ≥85% respectively. Bulk density increases with alumina content, from 1300 kg/m³ up to >1800 kg/m³. These inert ceramic balls provide excellent support for catalysts in fixed-bed reactors and serve as effective tower packing for gas/liquid contact processes.

Packaging Options
