Typical Applications

  • Desiccant and Dehydration: High-performance air drying, instrument air drying systems, and electronic components moisture control.
  • Industrial Equipment Compatibility: Ideal desiccant medium for heatless/heated compressed air dryers, air separation purifiers, PSA/VPSA systems, and high-purity nitrogen generators.
  • Chemical Carrier: Excellent choice as a robust catalyst carrier in various petrochemical processes.
